What You Will Learn

Business Basics +

Understand how to start a business and turn your ideas into something real. This module teaches you the fundamentals — business models, customer problems, the basics of money, and how to find the right idea — so you build on a solid foundation instead of guesswork.

How to Start +

Learn simple steps to move from having an idea to taking your first real action. We break it into clear, doable steps — validate the idea, name your business, register it, and launch your first offer — so you stop planning and start doing.
